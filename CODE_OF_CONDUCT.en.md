# PocketRoot Community Code of Conduct

[简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) | [English](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.en.md) | [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.en.md)

PocketRoot aims to provide a safe, respectful, and professional collaboration
environment for everyone. This policy applies to the repository, Issues, pull
requests, code reviews, and other public or private interactions made on behalf
of PocketRoot.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss facts, reproduction evidence, and engineering tradeoffs without
  attacking people.
- Respect different experience levels, languages, backgrounds, identities,
  and viewpoints.
- Give specific, actionable feedback and correct your own mistakes.
- Treat security, privacy, licensing, and upstream attribution carefully.
- Welcome new contributors and help them understand Experimental boundaries
  and validation gates.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, insults, stalking, or unwanted sexualized
  conduct.
- Publishing another person's private information, credentials, signing
  material, or device data.
- Intentionally submitting malicious code, unauthorized binaries or RootFS
  payloads, or material with unknown licensing provenance.
- Repeatedly disrupting discussion, impersonating others, retaliating against
  reporters, or evading maintainer enforcement.
- Misrepresenting engineering validation as production, legal, App Review, or
  distribution approval.

## Enforcement

Routine disagreement should stay calm and return to technical evidence. Report
conduct concerns privately to the repository owner's public contact,
[`jacklvapple@gmail.com`](mailto:jacklvapple@gmail.com); do not open a public
Issue. Include links, dates, participants, and necessary context without
redistributing unrelated personal information.

Maintainers will preserve confidentiality when practical and may issue a
warning, edit or remove content, temporarily restrict participation, or impose
a permanent ban according to severity. A maintainer with a conflict of interest
must recuse themselves. Good-faith reports are not penalized when no violation
is ultimately found; knowingly false or harassing reports may themselves
violate this policy.
